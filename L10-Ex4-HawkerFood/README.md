# L10-Ex4-HawkerFood - Week 5 Lesson 10 Exercise 4 (GRADED: app + demo video to PoliteMall + GitHub by Sat 23:59)
Asked: a new list app from scratch on a topic different from every other app this module
(no Pokemon, animals or boats), at least 2 sections with at least 2 items each, every item with
an image, styled with StyleSheets + Flexbox. Plus a quick demo video talking about the app.
Done (App.js): "Hawker Favourites", a SectionList of Singapore hawker food.
- 3 sections: Rice & Noodles (4), Snacks & Sides (4), Desserts (2), each header with its own
  colour, a MaterialCommunityIcons icon and an item count.
- Each item is a white card (`flexDirection: 'row'`): photo on the left, name, short description
  and price on the right. Tap an item for an Alert with details.
- Images are bundled locally in `img/` (no internet needed). Photos are from Wikimedia Commons
  (the Wikipedia article images for each dish, CC licensed), resized to 480px wide. Prices are
  typical example prices.
